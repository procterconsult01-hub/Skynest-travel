Addis Ababa

Highland capital — coffee, culture and African diplomacy

Why Addis Ababa?

Africa’s diplomatic capital sits at 2,355 m with cool highland air, exceptional coffee culture, important museums, and a strong sense of Ethiopian identity. Ideal base for exploring the historic north or the Rift Valley.

Getting There

Airport: Bole International (ADD). Major African and Middle Eastern hub with growing long-haul network.
