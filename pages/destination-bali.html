Island of the Gods — beaches, temples, rice terraces & wellness

Getting There

Main gateway: Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in Denpasar. Direct flights from major Asian hubs (Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Hong Kong, Tokyo) and some long-haul from Australia, Middle East and Europe. Average flight time from US West Coast: 18–22 hours with 1 stop.

Where to Stay

  • Seminyak / Canggu — Beach clubs, cafes, digital nomad vibe
  • Ubud — Culture, yoga, rice fields, quieter
  • Uluwatu — Cliffs, surf, luxury resorts
  • Nusa Dua — Family-friendly resorts, calm beaches
