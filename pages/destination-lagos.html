Lagos

Africa’s largest city — pure energy, creativity and ambition

Why Lagos?

The commercial and cultural powerhouse of Nigeria. Expect traffic, intensity, world-class Afrobeats, art, fashion, and some of the best food on the continent. Not a relaxed beach holiday — an unforgettable urban experience.

Getting There

Airport: Murtala Muhammed International (LOS). Major West African hub with long-haul links to Europe, Middle East and the US.
