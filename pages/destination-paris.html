Paris

The City of Light

Why Paris?

Art, architecture, food, fashion and atmosphere. From the Louvre and Eiffel Tower to hidden courtyards and neighborhood cafés, Paris rewards both first-time visitors and return travelers.

Getting There

Airports: Charles de Gaulle (CDG) and Orly (ORY). Excellent connections from almost everywhere. Eurostar arrives at Gare du Nord from London.

Where to Stay

  • Le Marais — Historic, trendy, central
  • Saint-Germain — Classic Left Bank charm
  • Latin Quarter — Lively, student energy, near Notre-Dame
  • Montmartre — Village feel and views
  • Opera / Grands Boulevards — Convenient for shopping & transport
