Windhoek

Gateway to the Namib, Etosha and epic self-drive landscapes

Why Windhoek?

Small, orderly capital and the perfect starting point for Namibia’s famous self-drive routes: Sossusvlei dunes, Etosha National Park, Skeleton Coast and more.

Getting There

Airport: Hosea Kutako International (WDH). Connections via Johannesburg, Cape Town, Frankfurt and regional hubs.
