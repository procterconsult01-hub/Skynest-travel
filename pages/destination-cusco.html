Cusco

Inca capital and gateway to Machu Picchu

Why Cusco?

High-altitude former capital of the Inca Empire. Cobblestone streets, colonial churches built on Inca foundations, and the essential base for Machu Picchu, the Sacred Valley and the Inca Trail.

Getting There

Airport: Alejandro Velasco Astete (CUZ). Most travelers fly from Lima (1 hour). Acclimatize for a day or two before heavy activity.
