Victoria Falls

One of the Seven Natural Wonders — thunder and adventure

Why Victoria Falls?

The world’s largest sheet of falling water. Livingstone (Zambia) and Victoria Falls town (Zimbabwe) offer viewing points, white-water rafting, bungee, microlight flights and easy safari extensions.

Getting There

Airports: Harry Mwanga Nkumbula (LVI) in Livingstone or Victoria Falls Airport (VFA). Both have regional and some international connections.
