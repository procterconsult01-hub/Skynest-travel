Zanzibar

Spice Island — Stone Town, beaches & Swahili soul

Why Zanzibar?

Historic Stone Town, powder-white beaches, turquoise water, spice plantations and a perfect post-safari recovery destination.

Getting There

Airport: Abeid Amani Karume International (ZNZ). Direct flights from Nairobi, Dar es Salaam, Johannesburg, and seasonal European charters. Many travelers fly in after safari in mainland Tanzania or Kenya.

Where to Stay

  • Stone Town — History, alleys, culture
  • Nungwi / Kendwa — Best beaches and swimming
  • Paje / Jambiani — East coast, kitesurfing, quieter
  • Matemwe / Mnemba area — Upscale and diving
