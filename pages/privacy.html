Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

SkyNest (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

When you use SkyNest we may collect:

  • Search queries (origins, destinations, dates) to provide relevant results
  • Basic device and browser information for performance and security
  • Contact details you voluntarily submit via our contact form

2. How We Use Information

  • To power search and direct you to airline or hotel partners
  • To improve the website and fix issues
  • To respond to your messages
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties

3. Third-Party Services

When you click through to book, you leave SkyNest and interact directly with the airline or hotel website. Their privacy policies apply. We may use privacy-friendly analytics (e.g. anonymized traffic stats) that do not identify individual users.

4. Cookies

We use only essential cookies needed for the site to function. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies by default.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Contact us at hello@skynest.travel to exercise these rights.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@skynest.travel.
