Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

SkyNest (“we”, “us”) is a travel discovery website. This policy explains, in plain terms, what information is involved when you use the site and what we do with it.

1. What we collect

  • Messages you send us. Our contact form opens your own email app; we receive only what you choose to email us.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) automatically processes standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.
  • Search inputs. Destinations and dates you type into search tools are used to build a link to a partner site. We do not create accounts or store booking or payment details.

2. Contact form data

The contact form does not store anything on this website. It prepares an email in your own email app, and messages arrive in our inbox. We use them only to reply to you. We keep messages for as long as needed to deal with your request and then delete them. We never sell or rent your details.

3. Cookies and analytics

SkyNest itself does not set advertising cookies. The site may store small preferences in your browser (local storage) so pages work properly. If we add analytics, we will use aggregated traffic statistics to understand which pages are useful, and we will update this page to name the provider.

When you click a partner link, the partner (or the affiliate network) may set its own cookies to record that you came from SkyNest. Those cookies are governed by the partner’s own privacy policy. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Affiliate links

Some links on SkyNest are, or may become, affiliate links. We may join programs such as Travelpayouts (which includes partners such as Aviasales and Hotellook), Booking.com, Viator, Expedia, Discover Cars, and similar travel booking programs. If you book through one of these links we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

5. Third-party sites

Bookings are made on airline, hotel, tour and car rental partner websites, not on SkyNest. Once you leave our site, the partner’s terms and privacy policy apply.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you (for example, an email you sent us). To make a request, use our contact page or email Procterconsult01@gmail.com.

7. Children

SkyNest is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
