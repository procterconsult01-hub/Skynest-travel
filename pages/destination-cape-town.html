Cape Town

Where the mountains meet the ocean

Why Cape Town?

Dramatic scenery, vibrant food scene, world-class wine, penguins, and the iconic Table Mountain. One of the most beautiful cities on Earth and a perfect gateway to the rest of South Africa.

Getting There

Airport: Cape Town International (CPT). Direct flights from major African hubs, Middle East and some European cities. Most long-haul travelers connect via Johannesburg, Dubai, Doha or Istanbul.

Where to Stay

  • V&A Waterfront — Convenient, restaurants, shops, easy airport transfer
  • Camps Bay / Clifton — Beach lifestyle and sunset views
  • City Bowl / Gardens — Central, walkable, near Table Mountain cableway
  • Constantia — Quiet wine-estate area
