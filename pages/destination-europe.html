Europe

Cities of art, history, food and endless train journeys

Why Europe?

Compact distances, legendary high-speed trains, world-class museums, diverse cuisine and something new around every corner. Whether it’s a romantic weekend in Paris or a multi-country rail adventure, Europe rewards every style of traveler.

Getting Around

High-speed trains (TGV, Frecciarossa, AVE, Eurostar) make multi-city trips effortless. Consider a Eurail / Interrail pass for longer journeys. Budget airlines (Ryanair, easyJet, Wizz) connect secondary cities cheaply if you travel light.

Where to Stay

  • Boutique hotels in historic centers
  • Apartments for longer stays (great kitchens & local living)
  • Design hotels in Barcelona, Amsterdam, Copenhagen
  • Classic grand hotels in Paris & Rome for special occasions

  • Central Europe: Berlin → Prague → Vienna → Budapest
