Amman

Gateway to Petra, the Dead Sea and Wadi Rum

Why Amman?

Jordan’s friendly capital and the practical base for the country’s greatest hits: Petra, the Dead Sea, Wadi Rum desert and Jerash. Modern cafés, Roman ruins and warm hospitality.

Getting There

Airport: Queen Alia International (AMM). Good connections from Europe, the Gulf and the wider Middle East.
