Cairo

Pyramids, Nile & the world’s greatest open-air museum

Why Cairo?

Home to the last remaining Wonder of the Ancient World, a chaotic and fascinating metropolis on the Nile, and the starting point for most Egypt itineraries.

Getting There

Airport: Cairo International (CAI). Major hub with excellent connections from Europe, Middle East, Africa and beyond.

Where to Stay

  • Zamalek / Garden City — Quieter, leafy, central
  • Downtown / Tahrir — Near museum and Nile
  • Giza / Pyramid area — For pyramid views (further from center)
  • Nile-view hotels — Classic big-city experience
