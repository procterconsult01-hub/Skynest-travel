Marrakech

The Red City — souks, riads & desert gateways

Why Marrakech?

Intense colors, labyrinthine medina, rooftop terraces, and easy access to the High Atlas and Sahara. One of North Africa’s most magnetic destinations.

Getting There

Airport: Marrakech Menara (RAK). Direct flights from many European cities; connections via Casablanca, Istanbul, Dubai or Doha for long-haul.

Where to Stay

  • Medina riads — Authentic courtyard houses, best atmosphere
  • Gueliz / Hivernage — Modern area, easier logistics
  • Palmeraie — Resort-style stays on the edge of the city
