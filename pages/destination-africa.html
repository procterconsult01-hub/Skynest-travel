Africa

Safari, vibrant cities, ancient cultures & endless horizons

Why Africa?

From the savannas of the Serengeti to the colorful medinas of Morocco and the dramatic coastline of Cape Town, Africa offers some of the world’s most unforgettable travel experiences. Whether you seek wildlife, culture, beaches or adventure, the continent has it all.

Getting There

Major gateways: Cape Town (CPT), Johannesburg (JNB), Nairobi (NBO), Casablanca (CMN), Addis Ababa (ADD). Many European and Middle Eastern carriers offer one-stop connections. Direct flights from select US cities are limited but growing.

Where to Stay

  • Safari lodges & tented camps — for wildlife experiences
  • Riads in Marrakech — traditional courtyard houses
  • Boutique hotels in Cape Town — city & waterfront
  • Beach resorts in Zanzibar — post-safari recovery
