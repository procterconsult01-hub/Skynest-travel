Rome

The Eternal City

Why Rome?

Layers of history at every turn — Colosseum, Forum, Vatican, piazzas, gelato and trattorias. Rome is intense, beautiful and endlessly walkable in the historic center.

Getting There

Airport: Fiumicino (FCO) is the main international gateway. Ciampino (CIA) serves low-cost carriers. Trains and buses connect both to the center.

Where to Stay

  • Centro Storico — Most atmospheric, walking distance to major sights
  • Trastevere — Nightlife and Roman neighborhood feel
  • Monti — Trendy, near Colosseum
  • Prati — Quieter, near Vatican

Flight + Hotel + Car Packages
