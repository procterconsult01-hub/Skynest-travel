Kampala

Friendly capital on seven hills — gateway to gorillas and the Nile

Why Kampala?

Uganda’s lively, welcoming capital. Green hills, markets, nightlife, and the main base for mountain gorilla trekking in Bwindi or Mgahinga, plus access to the source of the Nile at Jinja.

Getting There

Airport: Entebbe International (EBB), about 40 km from the city. Regional and some long-haul connections.
