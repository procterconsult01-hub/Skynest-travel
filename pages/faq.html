Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about searching and booking with SkyNest.

Is SkyNest a booking agency?

No. SkyNest is a discovery platform. We help you find flights and hotels, then link you directly to the airline or hotel’s official site to complete the booking. You book and pay with them — we never process payments or issue tickets.

Are the prices accurate?

The prices you see after clicking through are the live prices from Google Flights, Booking.com or the airline itself. We do not add markups.

Do you charge any fees?

SkyNest itself is free to use. Any fees you see are those charged by the airline or hotel (baggage, seat selection, resort fees, etc.).

Can I change or cancel a booking?

All changes and cancellations must be handled directly with the airline or hotel you booked with. Check their cancellation policy on their website.

How do I contact support?

For questions about using SkyNest, email us at hello@skynest.travel or use the contact form. For issues with a booking, contact the airline or hotel directly.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We collect minimal data and do not sell personal information. See our full Privacy Policy.
