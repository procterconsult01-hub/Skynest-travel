Rio de Janeiro

Beaches, mountains and Carnival spirit

Why Rio?

One of the world’s most spectacular city settings — mountains plunging into the sea, iconic beaches, Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf and an infectious energy that peaks during Carnival.

Getting There

Airport: Galeão (GIG) for international; Santos Dumont (SDU) for domestic. Direct flights from many US, European and South American cities.
