Dubai

Futuristic skyline, desert and luxury

Why Dubai?

Record-breaking architecture, world-class shopping, desert adventures and a global hub that makes an ideal stopover or standalone luxury destination.

Getting There

Airport: Dubai International (DXB) — one of the world’s busiest. Excellent connections almost everywhere. Al Maktoum (DWC) also serves some flights.
