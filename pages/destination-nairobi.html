Nairobi

Safari capital of East Africa

Why Nairobi?

The only major city with a national park on its doorstep. Nairobi is the main jump-off for Masai Mara, Amboseli, Laikipia and the Kenyan coast.

Getting There

Airport: Jomo Kenyatta International (NBO). Excellent connections across Africa, Europe, Middle East and Asia.
