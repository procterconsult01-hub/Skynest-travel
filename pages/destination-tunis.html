Why Tunis?

North African capital with a beautiful medina, the ruins of Carthage, the blue-and-white village of Sidi Bou Said, and easy access to Roman sites and Mediterranean beaches.

Getting There

Airport: Tunis–Carthage (TUN). Excellent connections from Europe and the Maghreb.
