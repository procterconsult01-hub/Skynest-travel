Fez

The spiritual and cultural heart of Morocco

Why Fez?

Home to one of the world’s largest and most intact medieval medinas. Tanneries, madrasas, souks and an atmosphere that feels centuries old. Less touristy than Marrakech, more intense and authentic.

Getting There

Airport: Fès–Saïs (FEZ). Domestic and some European flights; many travelers arrive by train or car from Casablanca or Marrakech.
