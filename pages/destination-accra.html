Accra

Vibrant West African capital — markets, music & coastal history

Why Accra?

One of West Africa’s most dynamic cities — friendly energy, excellent food, lively nightlife, and easy access to Cape Coast castles and the beautiful beaches of the Ghanaian coast.

Getting There

Airport: Kotoka International (ACC). Good connections from Europe, the Middle East, and other African hubs.
