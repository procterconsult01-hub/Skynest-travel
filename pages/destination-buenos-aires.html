Buenos Aires

Tango, steakhouses and European-style grandeur

Why Buenos Aires?

Paris of South America with passionate tango, world-class beef, beautiful neighborhoods (Palermo, Recoleta, San Telmo) and a late-night culture that never really sleeps.

Getting There

Airport: Ezeiza (EZE) for international; Aeroparque (AEP) for domestic and regional. Excellent long-haul connections.
